# Relay scene — design decisions

Where the implementation departs from `docs/concept.md`, or settles something the concept leaves open, and why.

- 2026-10-22 — The scene is a fixed backdrop under the whole home page: `#scene-root` switches from absolutely positioned inside the hero to `position: fixed` once the scene mounts, and a scrim (`.scene-scrim`, `--scene-bg` gradient) dims the scene under the text column — the concept re-forms the network per section, so it must stay visible below the hero, and Chromium culls a fixed layer inside a scrolled-away absolute box
- 2026-10-22 — Node counts sit well below the concept's performance budget (760 desktop, 440 phone instead of ~1–1.5k / ~600); the quality test now treats the concept numbers as a ceiling — at 1400 nodes the hero read as a dense plexus, against "silence as the base state"
- 2026-10-22 — No SVG still is shown while three.js loads; the canvas fades in over the page background — a 2D still cross-fading into a different 3D layout read as a glitch and costs paint before LCP
- 2026-10-22 — Under `prefers-reduced-motion` a section change cuts to the new static frame instead of a short cross-fade — the reduced-motion tokens collapse every duration to 1 ms by design
- 2026-10-22 — Offer and project cards are 72 % opaque so the clusters and constellations behind them stay perceptible; on wide screens the three service clusters sit behind the three offer columns, so a hovered offer lights the cluster behind it — text contrast was measured from rendered pixels instead of tokens (p95 background ≥ 4.62:1 over 817 text runs, desktop and mobile, at every scroll step)
- 2026-10-22 — Metric-matched local fallback fonts (`size-adjust`, ascent/descent overrides) added to fonts.css and the font stacks — removes a CLS of 0.34 on /en/ mobile that already existed on main and kept Performance at 79, below the required 85
- 2026-10-22 — The scene runs on the two home pages only; case-study pages have no scene mount, so the concept's dimmed "background mode" for content pages is not implemented here
- 2026-10-22 — `renderer.ts`, `shaders.ts`, `app.ts` and `index.ts` have no unit tests — every decision they take is extracted into pure, tested modules (network, layouts, morph, routing, quality, input, still); the GPU and DOM parts are verified by Playwright screenshots, frame-time sampling and Lighthouse in Chromium
- 2026-10-22 — Not verified here: the concept's 5-second recognition test with three people, and frame times on a physical mid-range phone (measured on a laptop GPU and in mobile emulation only)
- 2026-10-22 — A resting cursor (or a tap) places an order at the nearest node: a task travels out from it and the result comes back to it — the concept says the network "sends a result to it"; showing the outgoing task first gives the returning result a visible cause
- 2026-10-22 — Four draw calls instead of three: a faint cursor-glow sprite is a separate quad — one extra call with a single quad is cheaper than folding the glow into every node fragment
- 2026-10-22 — "Pause off screen" can only trigger when the canvas is not rendered at all (print, a collapsed viewport), because the scene is a fixed backdrop; the effective pause is the hidden tab (`visibilitychange`)
- 2026-10-22 — On touch screens (no hover) the offer or project card crossing the middle of the viewport is the active card that lights its cluster or constellation
- 2026-10-22 — A last section shorter than half the viewport (contact on tall screens) also becomes active once it is fully in view, so convergence happens even when it never reaches the middle line
